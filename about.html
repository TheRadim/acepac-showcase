About ACEPAC

Built by riders who wanted to stay on the trail after dusk.

ACEPAC describes itself simply: riders with travel ambition, testing bags on rugged terrain and building around the way they actually ride.

Shop bikepacking bags

Durability and ecology

A product that lasts a long time is ecological.

The development pillars are durable design, replaceable components and simple use. ACEPAC leans into sewn construction because seams are repairable, inspectable and service-friendly.

Service

Repair the bag, keep the memories.

If an ACEPAC product is damaged, the service approach is to repair it for the whole period of use. The brand also states a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.
